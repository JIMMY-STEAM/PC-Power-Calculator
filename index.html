How the estimate works

1. Enter average watts

Use the average power drawn while the PC is on. A wall meter gives a better estimate than adding component or power supply ratings.

2. Add your routine

Enter hours used each day and how many days each week the PC is running. Add more devices if you want a combined estimate.

3. Use your tariff

Enter the price per kilowatt-hour from your bill. The calculator excludes standing charges and other parts of the bill.

Formula: energy (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1,000. Cost = energy × your price per kWh. The monthly figure uses your selected month length and days per week.
